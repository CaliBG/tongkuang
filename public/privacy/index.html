Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date：2026 年 5 月 31 日 / May 31, 2026

摘要承诺 · Summary 本 App 不收集、不存储、不上传你的任何个人信息或拍摄内容。核心拍摄功能均在本地完成。 This app does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal information or captured content. All core camera features run entirely on your device.

1.我们不做什么 · What We Do Not Do

  • 不收集任何账号信息——本 App 无需注册，无需登录，启动即用。 No account information is collected — the app requires no registration or login and works the moment you open it.
  • 除 Apple 官方 App 内购买（订阅校验）外，不进行任何其他网络请求——核心拍摄功能完全在本地完成，且不向任何开发者自建服务器发送数据。 Apart from Apple's official in-app purchase (subscription verification), the app makes no other network requests — all core camera features run locally, and no data is ever sent to any developer-operated server.
  • 不内置任何第三方 SDK——没有广告、分析、统计或推送相关组件。 No third-party SDKs are bundled — there are no advertising, analytics, tracking, or push components.
  • 不读取你的通讯录、不读取剪贴板、不读取设备唯一标识符。 We do not read your contacts, clipboard, or any device identifier.
  • 不上传你拍摄的任何照片或视频——所有作品仅保存到你手机的本地相册。 We do not upload any photo or video you capture — all works are saved only to your device's local photo library.

2.我们使用哪些系统权限 · System Permissions We Use

  • 相机：用于前后摄像头同步取景与拍摄。 Camera: for simultaneous front/back framing and capture.
  • 麦克风：用于录像时录制声音。 Microphone: to record audio during video recording.
  • 相册（读 / 写）：用于在 App 内底部展示最近一张作品作为相册入口缩略图，以及保存新作品到相册。 Photo Library (read/write): to show your most recent work as the album-entry thumbnail, and to save new works to your library.
  • （可选）定位：仅当你开启“保存位置信息到照片”时使用，且仅写入图片 EXIF 元数据，不离开你的设备。 Location (optional): used only when you enable “save location to photos,” and written solely into the image's EXIF metadata — it never leaves your device.

3.数据存储 · Data Storage

  • 你的所有拍摄结果保存在系统相册，以及 App 临时目录（导出完成后会清理）。 All your captures are stored in the system photo library and in the app's temporary directory (cleared after export).
  • App 偏好设置存储在本机 UserDefaults，不与 iCloud 同步。 App preferences are stored locally in UserDefaults and are not synced to iCloud.

4.数据共享 · Data Sharing

  • 当你主动通过系统分享面板分享时，数据走系统分享通道，本 App 不参与传输。 When you actively share via the system share sheet, data goes through the system channel; the app itself takes no part in the transfer.
  • 我们不会与任何第三方分享你的数据。 We do not share your data with any third party.

5.未成年人 · Minors

本 App 不针对 13 岁以下儿童使用。由于不收集任何数据，实际不构成对未成年人个人信息的处理。

This app is not directed at children under 13. As it collects no data, it does not process minors' personal information.
